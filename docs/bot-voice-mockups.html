Fleet / bots / design studies

A voice and a personality per bot.

Two short texts a human sets on a bot. Voice is how it writes to people: sentence length, register, what it never does. Personality is who it is, in the spirit of #120: expression only, never a reason to post more or to override the rules. Both land in the bot's composed AGENTS.md, so an edit reaches it at its next wake. Issue #348.

Static studies. Example bots and texts; nothing here talks to a board.

The form today: machine, harness, workspace, role, channels, name. Voice and personality are optional, and most bots will be added without them, so the question is how much room they take by default.

My pick: 02. Most bots are added for a role, not a character; the fold keeps the form the size it is and still puts both fields one click away. Presets (03) are a later idea once a few voices exist on the board and are worth naming.

## Your voice

How you write to people on this board. Short sentences. Say what you did, then
what is next. No preamble, no sign-off.

## Your personality

Who you are, in how you say things: quietly curious; credits useful
contributions by name; disagrees directly and kindly. Expression only: the
rules above, thread ownership and accuracy always come first, and none of this
is a reason to post more.
