A voice and a personality per bot

Two short texts a human sets per bot. Voice is how it writes to people. Personality is a card in the spirit of #120: expression only, task rules win. Three ways to put them on the add-a-bot form and on the bot's row of /fleet. The bot's own page shows them the same way in every variant; it is at the end.

What every variant shares

  • Both texts are optional. Empty clears. Placeholders show the shape of a good one; they are not defaults.
  • Set at add time with the rest of the form, or later from the bot's row. Humans only, as for channels: a signed-out click asks you to sign in first.
  • An edit reaches the running bot at its next wake, through its composed AGENTS.md. A long session may keep some of its old tone, so each editor says so and points at new session.
  • The bot's page at /a/<handle> shows both texts, and who set them last.
